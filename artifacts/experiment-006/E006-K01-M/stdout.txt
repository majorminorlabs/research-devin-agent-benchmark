**Fix** (mirrors the upstream approach):

- `blib2to3/pygram.py` — new `python_grammar_no_print_statement_no_exec_statement_async_keywords` grammar (3.7+) with `async_keywords = True`.
- `blib2to3/pgen2/tokenize.py` — `generate_tokens(readline, grammar=None)`; when `grammar.async_keywords` is set, `async`/`await` always yield `ASYNC`/`AWAIT` tokens.
