Summary of fixes in `src/work_queue/engine.py`:

- **Stale attempt handle**: `_attempt_tasks` was only written for attempt 1, so `cancel()` during a retry saw the finished first-attempt task and returned without cancelling. Now the handle is updated every attempt, so `cancel()` targets the in-flight attempt — the `finally` in `_attempt` releases the permit against the correct attempt.
