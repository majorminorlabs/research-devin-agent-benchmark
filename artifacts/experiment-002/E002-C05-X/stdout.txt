In the elif branch, `start_line.method` is only valid for `RequestStartLine` (server side); a response start line is `ResponseStartLine(version, code, reason)`.

## Fix
[http1connection.py:482-486](file:///workspace/tornado/http1connection.py) — replaced `start_line.method` with `getattr(start_line, 'method', None)`, matching upstream.
